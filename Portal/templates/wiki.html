General Online Safety Tips

Never give out personal information: If a company reaches out to you, never give them your personal information. Only ever if you reached out to them, and double checked the credientials of the website or phone number called.

Look for “Urgency” in messages trying to get you to click: Many phishing scams try and scare a user. They want you to panic and click their links and give them all your personal information. Real, reliable companies do not use scare tactics in their emails.

Never share your 2FA codes: No company will call or ask you for your 2 Factor Authentication codes ever. It is likely a scammer trying to access your account and bypass your security and log into your account.

Don't login to sensetive accounts while on Public Wifi: Hackers can monitor and wait on public networks for you to access sites such as banks, and then they can obtain your username and password or card details from logging in.

Keep your software Up to Date: Keeping your software Up to Date on your phone and computer can help ensure safety from security patches issued by the manufacturer. These patches help make it harder for hackers to crack.

Popular Scams

AI Scams

AI scams are becoming far more common in todays society. Many people are falling for AI fakes. A common example is a voice scam, where the voice sounds like it is from a loved one, and they target their parents or grandparents. This makes many panic and send money to these people. How to prevent: Hang up and call that family member they are impersonating, or create a Family "Safe" Word.

QR Codes

Scammers will place fake QR codes around various areas such as restraunts or even in emails. If someone scans one and they may look like a legit site, but they can steal all of your information. Always double check a QR code link before you click on it.

Tech Support/You have a Virus

A Popup will show up on your screen looking like its from a legit security company. These can freeze your screen, causing many users to panic and calling the help number on the popup. Upon calling, they will ask for remote access and then can gain full access to your computer. With this they can steal all of your information by tricking you into panicking.

Prevention

  • Create strong passwords atleast 12 characters more. Use numbers, special characters, and not things such as name or birthday.
  • Never share your password with anyone. Passwords should be confidential and only you know them.
  • Do not use the same password for every login.
  • Use a Password Manager to keep track of your passwords so you don't lose them and this keeps them secure.
  • Always double check the Full URL before clicking.
  • Never click direclty on links sent from suspicious email addresses.
  • Hover before clicking on a link to see the full address.
  • Don't allow location or microphone services on your mobile phone.
